KVD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE
OFFICIAL STUDENT AND ACADEMIC POLICY COMPENDIUM (2025-2026)

CHAPTER 1: ACADEMIC CREDITS & GRADUATION CRITERIA
1. Minimum Degree Requirements:
All undergraduate students must complete an aggregate of 160 academic credits over 8 regular semesters. A Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of not less than 6.00 on a 10.0 scale is mandatory to graduate. The maximum permissible window to complete the degree is 6 consecutive academic years.

2. Course Credit Distribution:
Basic Science Courses: 24 credits. Engineering Core Courses: 48 credits. Professional Electives: 18 credits. Open Electives: 12 credits. Humanities and Social Sciences: 12 credits. Mandatory Internships and Capstone Project: 16 credits.

3. Credit Transfer Regulations:
Students transferring from recognized institutions can transfer a maximum of 30 academic credits. Transferred credits must possess a minimum grade of 'B' (70% or Grade Point 7.0). Approval must be obtained from the Dean of Academic Affairs within the first 14 calendar days of the semester.

CHAPTER 2: ATTENDANCE, EXAMINATIONS & EVALUATION
1. Mandatory Attendance Policy:
Students are required to secure an attendance of 75% or higher in every registered theory and lab course to be permitted to sit for the Semester End Examination. 

2. Attendance Condonation:
Attendance between 65% and 74% may be condoned by the Vice-Chancellor solely on valid medical grounds. The candidate must submit an authentic hospital discharge summary within 3 business days of return. Any attendance lower than 65% results in a Grade 'SA' (Shortage of Attendance), requiring the student to repeat the entire course in a subsequent semester.

3. Assessment Breakdown:
Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) carries 40% weightage. Semester End Examination (SEE) carries 60% weightage. To pass any course, a student must secure at least 45% in the SEE paper and 50% overall aggregate (CIE + SEE).

4. Letter Grading Thresholds:
Grade O (Outstanding): 90% - 100% (10.0 Grade Points)
Grade A+ (Excellent): 80% - 89% (9.0 Grade Points)
Grade A (Very Good): 70% - 79% (8.0 Grade Points)
Grade B+ (Good): 60% - 69% (7.0 Grade Points)
Grade B (Above Average): 55% - 59% (6.0 Grade Points)
Grade C (Pass): 50% - 54% (5.0 Grade Points)
Grade F (Fail): Below 50% (0.0 Grade Points)

CHAPTER 3: RESIDENTIAL HOSTEL RULES & MOVEMENT
1. Hostel Timings and Curfew:
The entrance gates of all on-campus hostels lock strictly at 20:30 on weekdays and 21:30 on weekends. Unauthorized late entry results in a warning on the first occurrence and parental notification on the second.

2. Night Outpass Process:
Students leaving campus for overnight stays must register an electronic outpass through the student ERP at least 24 hours prior to departure. Parental confirmation via SMS/Portal token verification is mandatory.

3. Prohibited Electrical Appliances:
Electric immersion water heaters, induction cookers, and heating coils are strictly barred from hostel rooms for electrical fire prevention. Unauthorized appliances will be confiscated.

CHAPTER 4: LIBRARY & DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE
1. Library Book Loans:
Undergraduate students can borrow 4 books simultaneously for up to 14 calendar days. A late return fee of Rs. 5 per day per book applies for the first 7 overdue days, escalating to Rs. 15 per day thereafter.

2. Campus Wi-Fi Usage:
Network access is restricted to 2 active client devices per enrolled student using institutional credentials. BitTorrent traffic, crypto mining, and unauthorized network scans trigger automatic MAC address blacklisting.

CHAPTER 5: CODE OF CONDUCT & ANTI-RAGGING
1. Zero Tolerance on Ragging:
Physical, verbal, or mental harassment is strictly prohibited. Violators face immediate suspension, rustication, and mandatory referral to law enforcement under statutory regulations.

2. Campus Speed Limit & Vehicles:
Motorized vehicles on campus roads must adhere to a strict speed limit of 20 km/h. First violation carries a fine of Rs. 500; repeat offenses lead to campus vehicle permit cancellation.
